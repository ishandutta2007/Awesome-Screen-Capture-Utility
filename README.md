# Awesome-Screen-Capture-Utility

## Top Screen Capture Utility Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Screenshot Capture, Screen Recording & Annotation Workflows*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial screen capture tools** and **open-source projects** that help users capture, annotate, edit, and share screenshots and screen recordings. These utilities range from lightweight snip tools to full-featured capture suites with OCR, scrolling capture, and cloud sharing.



**Examples** include Windows Snipping Tool, Snagit, Lightshot, Greenshot, ShareX, Gyazo, Loom, CleanShot X, ScreenRec, and CloudApp (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Screen capture is one of the strongest open-source domains. **ShareX** leads as the most feature-complete open-source capture suite with 30,000+ GitHub stars. **Flameshot** provides the best cross-platform annotation experience. **OBS Studio** dominates screen recording, while **Loom** alternatives like **Cap** and **Screenity** bring open-source video messaging. **Whistle** and **Cinnamon** offer modern browser-based capture. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Windows Snipping Tool](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/use-snipping-tool-to-capture-screenshots-00246869-1843-655f-f220-97299b865f6b)**  

  Microsoft's built-in screenshot utility for Windows 11 with **screen recording, window snip, and delayed capture** . **Free and pre-installed** — the default for Windows users . **Best for quick, basic captures** without installing third-party tools.



- **[Snagit](https://www.techsmith.com/screen-capture.html)**  

  **The industry standard for professional screen capture** — annotations, scrolling capture, OCR, video recording, and a built-in editor . **One-time purchase** ($62.99) or subscription . **The most feature-complete commercial tool** — used by technical writers, support teams, and educators.



- **[Lightshot](https://app.prntscr.com/)**  

  **The simplest screenshot tool** with instant sharing, basic annotation, and cloud upload . **Free with ads** . **Best for quick captures and sharing** — no account required.



- **[Greenshot](https://getgreenshot.org/)**  

  **The best lightweight Windows screenshot tool** — open-source (GPL) with annotation, OCR, and multiple export options . **Free and open-source** . **The standard for Windows users wanting ShareX-level features without complexity** .



- **[ShareX](https://getsharex.com/)**  

  **The most powerful open-source capture suite** — see Open-Source section below for full details.



- **[Gyazo](https://gyazo.com/)**  

  **Instant screenshot sharing** with GIF recording and cloud storage . **Free tier with limited history**; Pro for unlimited . **Best for quick visual communication** — captures, uploads, and copies URL in one click.



- **[Loom](https://www.loom.com/)**  

  **The leading async video messaging platform** — screen recording with webcam overlay, instant sharing, and viewer analytics . **Free tier with 5-minute limit**; Business at $15/month . **The standard for async communication** in remote teams.



- **[CleanShot X](https://cleanshot.com/)**  

  **The best macOS screenshot tool** — scrolling capture, OCR, annotation, screen recording, and cloud sharing . **One-time purchase** ($29) or subscription . **The macOS standard** for professional capture.



- **[ScreenRec](https://screenrec.com/)**  

  **Free screen recording and screenshot tool** with instant sharing and cloud storage . **Free forever** — monetized through premium features.



- **[CloudApp](https://www.getcloudapp.com/)**  

  **Screen recording and screenshot sharing** with annotation and analytics . **Subscription-based** ($9.95/month) . **Best for teams needing capture + sharing + analytics**.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[ShareX](https://github.com/ShareX/ShareX)**  

  **The most feature-complete open-source screen capture suite**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **30,000+ GitHub stars** . **Windows-only** but unmatched in capability: **screenshot, screen recording, GIF capture, scrolling capture, OCR, annotation, image editing, watermarking, and 80+ upload destinations** . **Automation workflows** — after-capture actions, hotkeys, and scheduled tasks . **The de facto open-source Snagit alternative** — free and more powerful than most commercial tools . **Best for power users** wanting maximum capture flexibility without subscription costs .



- **[Flameshot](https://github.com/flameshot-org/flameshot)**  

  **The best cross-platform screenshot tool with built-in annotation**, GPL-3.0 licensed with 25,000+ GitHub stars . **Linux, macOS, and Windows** support . **Intuitive annotation** — arrows, boxes, circles, text, blur, and highlight . **Customizable keyboard shortcuts and CLI** . **The standard for Linux users** — replaces GNOME Screenshot and Spectacle . **Best for quick captures with annotation** — simpler than ShareX but more polished .



- **[OBS Studio](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio)**  

  **The leading open-source screen recording and streaming platform**, GPL-2.0 licensed with **60,000+ GitHub stars** . **Not just screen capture** — professional video production with scenes, sources, transitions, and audio mixing . **Cross-platform** (Windows, macOS, Linux) . **The de facto standard for screen recording** — used by streamers, educators, and content creators . **Best for high-quality screen recording** with unlimited length and no watermarks .



- **[Cap](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap)**  

  **Open-source Loom alternative** for async screen recording and sharing, AGPL-3.0 licensed with 10,000+ GitHub stars . **Beautiful, lightweight, and shareable** — record screen with webcam overlay and share via link . **Cross-platform** (macOS, Windows, Linux in development) . **Best for async video messaging** without Loom's subscription .



- **[Screenity](https://github.com/alyssaxuu/screenity)**  

  **The most powerful open-source screen recorder for Chrome**, MIT licensed with 12,000+ GitHub stars . **Browser-based** — no installation beyond the extension . Features **screen recording, webcam overlay, annotation, and editing** . **The best open-source Loom alternative for browser workflows** . **Best for quick recordings without installing desktop software** .



- **[Whistle](https://github.com/whistle-io/whistle)**  

  **Modern open-source screenshot tool with OCR and AI-powered search**, MIT licensed . **Local-first** — screenshots stored on your device . **Search text within screenshots** via OCR . **Minimal and privacy-focused** . **Best for users wanting searchable screenshot archives** without cloud upload.



- **[Cinnamon](https://github.com/cinnamon-screenshot/cinnamon-screenshot)**  

  **Modern browser-based screenshot tool** with annotation, blur, and export options . **Open-source and lightweight** . **Best for quick browser captures** without desktop software .



- **[Ksnip](https://github.com/ksnip/ksnip)**  

  **Cross-platform screenshot tool with annotation and editing**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Qt-based** for Linux, Windows, and macOS . Features **rectangular/elliptical/freehand capture, annotation, and upload** . **Best for KDE/Qt users** wanting Flameshot-like features .



- **[Spectacle](https://github.com/KDE/spectacle)**  

  **KDE's screenshot utility**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Simple and integrated** with KDE Plasma . **Best for KDE users** wanting native capture without third-party tools .



- **[GNOME Screenshot](https://github.com/GNOME/gnome-screenshot)**  

  **GNOME's official screenshot tool**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Simple and clean** — basic capture with delay and effects . **Best for GNOME users** wanting default capture functionality .



- **[Lightscreen](https://github.com/ckaiser/Lightscreen)**  

  **Lightweight screenshot tool for Windows**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Simple and fast** — basic capture with auto-save and upload . **Best for users wanting minimal screenshot functionality** .



- **[Xfce4-screenshooter](https://github.com/xfce-mirror/xfce4-screenshooter)**  

  **Xfce's screenshot utility**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Integrated with Xfce** — simple capture with actions . **Best for Xfce users** .



- **[ScreenCloud](https://github.com/olav-st/screencloud)**  

  **Cross-platform screenshot tool with cloud upload**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Upload to multiple destinations** — Imgur, Dropbox, and custom servers . **Best for users wanting automatic cloud upload** from Linux .



### Screen Recording (Video)



- **[OBS Studio](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio)** — Already listed above. **The standard for screen recording** .

- **[SimpleScreenRecorder](https://github.com/MaartenBaert/ssr)** — **Lightweight Linux screen recorder**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Simple and fast** — best for basic screen recording on Linux .

- **[vokoscreenNG](https://github.com/vkohaupt/vokoscreenNG)** — **Modern screen recorder for Linux**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Simple and feature-rich** — best for tutorials and presentations .

- **[RecordMyDesktop](https://github.com/Enselic/recordmydesktop)** — **Classic Linux screen recorder**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Mature but dated** — best for legacy systems .

- **[Kazam](https://github.com/henrywoo/kazam)** — **Simple Linux screen recorder**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Lightweight** — best for quick captures .

- **[Peek](https://github.com/phw/peek)** — **Simple animated GIF screen recorder for Linux**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Best for quick GIF demos** .

**Frameworks for building custom screen capture solutions**: Choose based on platform and use case. **ShareX** for the most feature-complete Windows capture suite with 80+ upload destinations . **Flameshot** for cross-platform annotation-focused capture . **OBS Studio** for professional screen recording . **Cap** or **Screenity** for Loom-style async video . **Ksnip** for Qt-based cross-platform capture . **Whistle** for searchable screenshot archives with local-first privacy . Note that true enterprise screen capture with team libraries, analytics, and workflow automation remains primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong capture, annotation, and recording foundations that require integration for complete visual communication workflows.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Screen capture tools may capture sensitive information including passwords, personal messages, and confidential business data. **Review captures before sharing** — redact or blur sensitive content.

- **Cloud upload features transmit data to third-party servers** — Loom, Gyazo, and CloudApp store captures on their infrastructure. For sensitive content, use local-only tools like ShareX (with local save) or Whistle .

- **OBS Studio requires configuration** — while powerful, it's not a one-click screen recorder. SimpleScreenRecorder or vokoscreenNG are better for quick captures on Linux .

- **Wayland support varies** — Flameshot and some X11-based tools have limited Wayland compatibility. Check your display server before deployment .

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong capture, annotation, and recording foundations, but **team libraries, analytics, and enterprise workflow automation** remain primarily commercial offerings.
